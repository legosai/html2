Subject Descriptions

Mathematics & Logic: This subject focuses on developing problem-solving skills, logical reasoning, and mathematical concepts. Students will explore topics such as algebra, geometry, statistics, and critical thinking exercises.

English Literature & Creative Writing: In this subject, students will analyze literary works, explore different writing styles, and enhance their creative writing abilities. They will engage in reading comprehension, essay writing, and storytelling activities.

General Science & Lab: This subject introduces students to various scientific disciplines, including biology, chemistry, and physics. Students will conduct experiments, learn scientific principles, and develop an understanding of the natural world through hands-on activities in the laboratory.

History & World Cultures: In this subject, students will explore historical events, civilizations, and cultural diversity. They will analyze primary and secondary sources, engage in discussions, and develop an understanding of the impact of history on the present.

Computer Science & Coding: This subject focuses on programming, algorithms, and computational thinking. Students will learn coding languages, develop software applications, and explore the fundamentals of computer science through practical projects.

Physical Education & Health: This subject promotes physical fitness, wellness, and healthy lifestyle choices. Students will participate in various sports, exercise routines, and learn about nutrition, mental health, and overall well-being.

Creative Arts & Design: In this subject, students will explore different forms of artistic expression, including visual arts, music, and design. They will develop their creativity, learn about art history, and engage in hands-on projects to showcase their artistic skills.
